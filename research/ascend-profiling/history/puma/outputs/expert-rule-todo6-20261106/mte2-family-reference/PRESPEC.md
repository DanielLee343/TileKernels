# Pre-specified comparison
Input: existing TODO3A evidence captures, device record 0000:8B:00.0, rmsnorm and swiglu only. Each case is equally weighted. Group by shared source hash, using connected components if a capture has multiple hashes; missing hashes are unknown and excluded, never treated as independent. Leave one entire hash group out per fold within the same family. Fit the three 3A ordinary least-squares forms A=alpha*N, A=beta*G, A=gamma+alpha*N+beta*G, with its column scaling. Score case-equal median and P90 absolute relative errors, median signed error, negative predictions, and per-fold predictions. Report G and N ranges, correlation of their logarithms, and whether held-out G or N lies outside training ranges; this marginal range check does not prove interpolation in the joint feature space. No binning, tuning, or threshold; exploratory comparison only.
